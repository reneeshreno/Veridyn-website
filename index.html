About Us

Engineering-led, shop-built

We support clients across oil and gas, power, water treatment, renewable energy, data centers, and process industries with practical, in-house engineering and field-ready solutions.

Industries We Serve

Where our panels run

Serving oil & gas, power, chemical, petrochemical, renewable energy, water treatment, and thermal energy storage sectors.

INDUSTRY / 01

Oil & Gas

  • Skid and Package Automation — Control solutions for chemical injection, metering, pump, pressure-reduction and utility skids.
  • PLC, HMI, SCADA and ESD Systems — Reliable control, monitoring and emergency-shutdown systems for critical operations.
  • Instrumentation and Control Panels — Engineering and integration of field instruments, junction boxes, Remote I/O and custom control panels.
  • Testing and Lifecycle Support — FAT, SAT, commissioning, troubleshooting, modernization and ongoing technical support.
INDUSTRY / 02

Power

  • Power-Plant Auxiliary Automation — Control systems for pumps, cooling systems, fuel handling and balance-of-plant equipment.
  • PLC and SCADA Integration — Centralized monitoring, alarm management, sequencing and plant-system communication.
  • Control and Protection Panels — Engineered panels with interlocks, protection logic and reliable operator controls.
  • System Modernization — PLC migration, control-panel upgrades and digitalization of legacy power-generation systems.
INDUSTRY / 03

Renewable Energy & Data Centers

  • Renewable-Energy Automation — Control and monitoring solutions for solar, wind, battery-storage and hybrid-energy packages.
  • Critical Utility Control — Automation for cooling, pumping, fuel, water-treatment and backup-power systems.
  • Energy and Performance Monitoring — Real-time visualization, alarms, trends and operational data acquisition.
  • High-Availability Integration — Redundant control architectures and dependable interfaces for mission-critical facilities.
INDUSTRY / 04

Chemical & Petrochemical

  • Process Automation — PLC, HMI and SCADA solutions for dosing, blending, transfer and continuous process operations.
  • Safety and Interlocking Systems — ESD logic, permissives, alarms and shutdown sequences for hazardous processes.
  • Instrumentation Engineering — Selection and integration of flow, pressure, temperature, level and analytical instruments.
  • Packaged Process Systems — Complete automation for chemical injection, metering, pumping and utility skids.
INDUSTRY / 05

Waste Water Treatment

  • Treatment-Process Automation — Control solutions for screening, filtration, aeration, clarification, disinfection and sludge handling.
  • Pump and Dosing Control — Reliable sequencing and monitoring of transfer pumps, lift stations and chemical-dosing systems.
  • Remote Monitoring and SCADA — Centralized supervision, alarm management, trending and operational reporting.
  • Instrumentation Integration — Integration of flowmeters, level transmitters, analyzers, valves and water-quality instruments.
INDUSTRY / 06

Thermal Energy Storage

  • Charging and Discharging Control — Automated operating sequences for efficient thermal-energy storage and recovery.
  • Pump and Valve Automation — Precise control of circulation pumps, isolation valves and temperature-management equipment.
  • Performance Monitoring — Continuous tracking of temperature, flow, pressure, storage capacity and system efficiency.
  • Plant-System Integration — Seamless communication with HVAC, district-cooling, power-management and supervisory systems.
